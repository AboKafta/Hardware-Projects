**Summary of Game and Role:**

The game we created is a spinoff of Frog Launcher. The player is given 3 frogs and stands a certain distance away from the machine. There are three lily pads to land on and two alligators to avoid. The lily pads give either 1, 2 or 3 points based on the difficulty of landing on that pad. The alligators are -1. If four points are gotten with the three frog launches, then the game is won. Else, they lose and no coins are received. The game was made harder with roofs and trees in the away. My role on the team was the coder, but it expanded more as the project began.

**Pitfalls Ran into and What Should Have Been Done Differently:**

The first main pitfall was in the prototype. The code wasn’t working as intended, since when the button was pressed, it kept on adding to the count. I then asked Claude AI for help and helped me come up with a for loop to stop this.

The second and biggest pitfall was having to redo the circuitry after the prototype. The wires were too short and were not placed tightly. So, when it came to me having to fix the code, half the buttons weren’t working. I couldn’t see what wires were even connected or not because of the tape covering the extensions. Additionally, there was no wire management or any pictures to help identify the problem. So, I had to rewire the whole circuit, and the circuit person wasn’t responding to come help. It took around 3 hours to come up originally with the hardware part of the circuit, rewire it and finish any errors that occurred. Additionally, I thought I had burnt the pico but it turned out the big LED was using too much power, so we had to switch back to the small LED. If something had to be done differently, it’d be to manage the wires more properly and have the circuit person give more documentation on how the circuit was done.

Third obstacle was that the game was too easy. People were throwing the frog to the end of the board and using it as a backboard to score the hard lily pad. We got advice from Professor Davis so me and Arabella added a roof and a few obstacles to make it harder for milestone 4.

Fourth obstacle was the laser cutting. I went to FYELIC first and the holes were way too big. This is because the units in AutoCAD were inches and not cm so we had to fix that. You can see in the appendix this mistake. Plus, I laser cut the board for a big LED but we had to switch back to a small LED for power reasons.
